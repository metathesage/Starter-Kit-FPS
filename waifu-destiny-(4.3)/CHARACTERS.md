## 1. Codename: NOVA
**Title:** Silent Star | **Rarity:** ★★★★★ SSR  
**Class/Role:** Sniper Specialist / Recon (Star Ranger Archetype)  
**Visual Motif:** Halo Mark V Heavy Plating × Anime Tactical Undersuit  

### Profile Data
- **Age:** 21
- **Height:** 181 cm
- **Weight:** 58 kg
- **Specialties:** Ultra Long-Range Marksmanship, Optic Camouflage, Perimeter Infiltration

### Signature Equipment & Modules
- **Primary Weapon:** `S7 Sniper Rifle` (96.5 cm anti-material kinetic rail rifle)
- **Active Module:** `Motion Cloak Module` (invisibility while stationary or jet-dashing)
- **HUD Optic:** `Bio-Sensor Visor` (highlights enemy weakspots and structural fault lines through walls)
- **Helmet Design:** Enclosed Mark V angular helmet with asymmetric high-density ponytail exhaust port

## 2. Codename: LUX
**Title:** Platinum Halo Guardian | **Rarity:** ★★★★★ SSR  
**Class/Role:** Celestial Guardian / Vanguard (Vanguard Archetype)  
**Visual Motif:** Paladin Spartan × Forerunner Radiant Technology  

### Profile Data
- **Age:** 21
- **Height:** 172 cm
- **Traits:** Celestial Beauty, Divine Aegis, Unwavering Resolve
- **Appearance:** Twin-tail platinum blonde hair, wing-shaped ear-comms, regal white & gold etched plating, translucent crystalline hardlight cape.

### Signature Equipment & Abilities
- **Primary Weapon:** `Halo Lance` & `Divine Aegis Shield`
- **Ability 1 — Divine Aegis:** Deploys a convex golden barrier of hardlight that absorbs incoming fire and converts it into party shield regeneration.
- **Ability 2 — Astral Strike:** Calls down an orbital kinetic javelin from Aurelia’s upper atmosphere, dealing massive localized shockwave damage.
- **Ultimate — Halo Resonance:** Activates the crown hardlight halo, emitting a continuous wave of purification that staggers surrounding alien swarms and buffs allies.

**Visual Motif:** Carbon-Fibre Exosuit × Mechanical Floating Holographic Halo  

### Profile Data
- **Age:** 22
- **Height:** 175 cm
- **Traits:** Charismatic, Seductive, Calculated
- **Appearance:** Flowing crimson hair, fiery amber eyes with embedded combat HUD reticles, glossy obsidian stealth carapace with red glowing power conduits.

### Signature Equipment & Abilities
- **Signature Apparatus:** `Floating Mechanical Halo Rig` — An unattached, levitating electromagnetic ring emitter floating behind her head that channels and superheats relic energy.
- **Ability 1 — Infernal Halo Burst:** Summons a circular crimson hazard zone that incinerates enemies in high-density pulses.
- **Ability 2 — Glassfang Assault:** Hardens forearm armor into razor kinetic blades for high-velocity forward breach attacks.
- **Ultimate — Charm Protocol:** The halo emits a captivating electromagnetic frequency that halts enemy aggression, inflicts defense down, and causes targets to detonate upon death.
